About Me

My name is Hongxuan Chen, and I am an Assistant Professor (Teaching) in the School of Data Science at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. Before joining CUHKSZ, I completed my PhD and BS in Computer Science at University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. I was co-advised by Dr. Geoffrey Herman and Dr. Jeff Erickson, and my PhD dissertation was titled Investigating Student Difficulties and Evaluating Pedagogical Interventions for Graph Layering in Algorithm Design.
