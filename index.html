About Me

My name is Hongxuan Chen, and I am a fifth year PhD student in Computer Science at University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC). I am passionate about CS education, and my research focuses on how students learn algorithm design and discrete mathematics. I am co-advised by Dr. Geoffrey Herman and Dr. Jeff Erickson.

I am seeking teaching-track faculty jobs in the 2025-2026 academic year cycle. I had experience co-instructing Computer Architectures and Discrete Structures at UIUC, and I am capable of teaching Algorithms thanks to my research focus on those topics. I am also interested in teaching any intro-level CS course, as I am excited to welcome more students into CS and help them persist and thrive.

Here is my cv (last updated: September 2025) and Google Scholar profile.

Teaching

My teaching is grounded in constructivism, emphasizing active learning and exploration. I create welcoming classrooms where students feel comfortable asking questions, engaging with peers, and taking intellectual risks, supported by flexible policies that reduce stress. In both large flipped and smaller remote courses at UIUC, I used collaboration, comprehension checks, and active instructor presence to keep students engaged and connected. Besides, drawing on my computing education research, I identify common difficulties and design supports that help students build confidence as well as mastery of computer science concepts. To learn more, you can check my full teaching statement.
